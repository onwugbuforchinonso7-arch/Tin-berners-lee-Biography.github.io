• ENQUIRE (1980): While working as a software consultant at CERN (the European Particle Physics Laboratory) in Geneva, he built a prototype program called ENQUIRE that stored information using links, introducing the concept of hypertext.

• Proposal (1989): He wrote a proposal combining hypertext with the Internet to allow researchers globally to share information easily.
